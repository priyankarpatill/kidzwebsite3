Welcome to KidZ 'n' KidZZilla Play Zone

To ensure a safe and enjoyable environment for all children and families, we kindly request you to follow these rules and guidelines.

Important Notice

By entering the premises, parents/guardians acknowledge and agree to abide by the above Terms & Conditions & Play Zone Rules.

Must Read

1 General Terms

  • Entry is permitted only after registration/payment at the reception.
  • Children must always be accompanied by a parent/guardian unless enrolled in DayCare or supervised sessions.
  • KidZ 'n' KidZZilla is not responsible for the loss of personal belongings.
  • Management reserves the right to refuse entry or service to anyone who does not comply with the rules.

2 Safety Rules

  • Socks are mandatory for children and adults inside the Play Zone.
  • No outside food or beverages are allowed inside the play area.
  • Children who are sick, have contagious conditions, or show signs of illness will not be allowed inside.
  • Rough play, pushing, or inappropriate behavior is strictly prohibited.
  • Parents/guardians are responsible for their child's behavior and safety at all times.

3 Age & Usage Guidelines

  • Play Zone is designed for children aged 1 to 12 years.
  • Certain play equipment has age/height restrictions, which must be followed for safety.
  • Toddlers (under 3 years) should always be in the toddler-designated zone.

4 Food & Party Rules

  • Food and drinks are permitted only in designated areas (not inside play structures).
  • Party Hall bookings must be confirmed with an advance payment.
  • Decorations, cakes, and outside catering must be pre-approved by management.
  • Any damage to property during events/parties will be charged to the booking person.

5 DayCare & Tuition Rules

  • Children enrolled in DayCare/Tuitions must be signed in and signed out by an authorized parent/guardian.
  • Parents must inform staff about any allergies, medications, or special needs in advance.
  • Timely pick-up is expected; late fees may apply.

6 Payments, Refunds & Cancellations

  • All payments must be made in advance for entry, packages, or bookings.
  • Packages and passes are non-transferable and non-refundable.
  • Party booking cancellations made less than 7 days prior will not be eligible for refunds.

7 Photography & Media

  • Photos and videos may be taken by our team for promotional purposes only with parental consent.
  • Parents may take personal photos/videos of their children but should respect the privacy of other families.

8 Liability Disclaimer

  • While we take utmost care to provide a safe environment, KidZ 'n' KidZZilla is not liable for any injuries, accidents, or incidents resulting from unsafe play or parental negligence.
  • Parents/guardians accept full responsibility for their child's participation in play, daycare, and other activities.

9 Management Rights

  • Rules are subject to change for safety and operational reasons.
  • The decision of the management in all matters will be final.
