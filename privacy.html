Privacy Policy

Your child's safety, enjoyment, and privacy are our top priorities at KidZ 'n' KidZZilla Play Zone

1 Information We Collect

At KidZ 'n' KidZZilla, we collect necessary information to provide safe and enjoyable experiences for your children:

  • Personal Details: Parent/guardian name, contact number, email, and address for registrations and bookings.
  • Child's Information: Name and age, required to provide age-appropriate play and daycare services.
  • Payment Details: Collected securely when purchasing entry passes, packages, or booking events.
  • Media (Optional): Photos or videos taken during parties/events (only with parental consent).

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information responsibly to enhance your experience with us:

  • To confirm bookings, registrations, and payments.
  • To ensure the safety, security, and supervision of children at the Play Zone.
  • To provide updates on events, offers, and new services.
  • To improve our facilities and customer experience.

3 Sharing of Information

We value your privacy and handle your information with care:

  • We do not sell or share your information with unrelated third parties.
  • Limited information may be shared with service providers (e.g., payment gateways, communication providers).
  • Information may be disclosed to legal authorities if required by law.

4 Safety & Security

We prioritize the safety and security of all our visitors:

  • CCTV surveillance is maintained in the Play Zone for the safety of children and families.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized staff only.
  • Online transactions are protected through secure, encrypted systems.

5 Parent & Guardian Responsibility

We work together with parents to ensure children's safety:

  • Children must be accompanied by a parent/guardian unless they are enrolled in daycare or supervised activities.
  • Accurate information must be provided during registration for safety and communication purposes.
